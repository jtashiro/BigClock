BigClock Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

BigClock is a full-screen clock app that shows the current time, local weather, and the current Bitcoin price. This page explains what data the app uses and how.

Location

BigClock uses your device's location, or a zip/postal code and country you enter in the app's settings, solely to look up current weather conditions from the U.S. National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). Location data is used only to make this weather request and is not stored, logged, or sent anywhere else. If you enter a zip/postal code instead, that code and the country you paired it with are saved on your device (in the app's local settings) so the app can reuse it on future launches; this information never leaves your device except as part of the weather lookup itself.

Weather data

Weather conditions are fetched from the National Weather Service (api.weather.gov), a free U.S. government service. No account or API key is required, and BigClock does not send any personal information to this service beyond the coordinates needed to identify a location.

Bitcoin price

BigClock fetches the current Bitcoin price from Coinbase's public spot price API (api.coinbase.com), which does not require an account or API key. This request does not include any information about you or your device.

What BigClock does not do

  • No account creation or sign-in
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties

Permissions

BigClock requests location permission only to support the weather feature described above. You can deny this permission and instead enter a zip/postal code in the app's Weather Location settings.
